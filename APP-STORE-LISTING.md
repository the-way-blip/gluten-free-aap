## Description (4000 char max)

Going gluten-free can feel overwhelming at first — the label-reading, the
second-guessing at restaurants, the "what can I even make tonight?" Sift is the
warm, practical companion that makes it feel doable again.

Whether you're newly diagnosed, cooking for someone who is, or just feel better
without gluten, Sift meets you where you are and adapts to how careful you need
to be.

**Cook without the guesswork**
• Gluten-free recipes filtered to your needs — by time, difficulty, and
  ingredients you actually have
• "What can I make?" turns your pantry into dinner ideas
• Adjust any recipe to the servings you're cooking for — amounts update for you
• Mark dairy-free too, and set your own strictness

**Shop smarter**
• Build a smart grocery list, grouped by store or by recipe
• Add a whole recipe's missing items in one tap
• Share your list with a partner, or take it to the store

**Eat out with confidence**
• Personalized gluten-free guidance for popular restaurants, tuned to your
  cross-contamination comfort level
• Save your favorite "safe spots" and share them with friends
• Quick ordering tips so you can relax at the table

**Check before you buy**
• Look up ingredients and products for likely gluten
• Snap a photo of your pantry or fridge to add items fast

**Made to feel calm, not clinical**
• A warm, hand-painted design — not another sterile health app
• Works offline-friendly and saves to your device
• Optional account to sync across your phone and tablet

Sift is your gluten-free sidekick for real life — the weeknight dinners, the
group brunches, the road trips. Less stress, more good food.

—

Sift provides general gluten-free guidance and is not medical advice. Always
confirm ingredients with current labels and restaurant staff. If you have
celiac disease, a severe allergy, or other medical concerns, consult a
qualified healthcare professional.

Premium unlocks unlimited AI recipe ideas, the ingredient checker, pantry photo
scan, and cross-device sync, after a free trial. Subscriptions auto-renew;
manage or cancel anytime in your account settings.
